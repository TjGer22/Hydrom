# Setting Up Temperature Compensation

## What is Temperature Compensation?

The Hydrom hydrometer is calibrated at 20°C (68°F) during manufacturing. However, during fermentation, your beer or wine may be at a different temperature—warmer or cooler. Since the density of liquids changes with temperature, this difference can introduce small measurement errors.

**Temperature compensation automatically corrects these errors** by using the Hydrom's built-in temperature sensor to adjust readings based on the actual liquid temperature. This ensures you get accurate gravity measurements regardless of fermentation conditions.

!!! note
    The sensor must be fully submerged for accurate temperature readings. Make sure the Hydrom floats freely in your fermenter.

## How to Enable Temperature Compensation

### Step 1: Open the Menu

Open the Hydrom web interface in your browser. Look for the **menu icon** (three horizontal lines) in the top-left corner of the screen.

![Open the menu](../assets/images/Folie4.png)

### Step 2: Navigate to Settings

Tap or click on **Settings** from the menu options that appear.

### Step 3: Enable Temperature Compensation

In the Settings page, locate the **Temperature Compensation** toggle switch. Tap or click to enable it.

![Save your settings](../assets/images/Folie5.png)

### Step 4: Save Your Changes

Click the **Save** button to apply the temperature compensation setting. You'll see a confirmation message indicating the setting has been saved.

## What to Expect

Once enabled, the Hydrom will:

- **Monitor liquid temperature** continuously using its built-in sensor
- **Automatically adjust** gravity readings to reflect 20°C equivalent values
- **Display both** the actual reading and temperature in the web interface
- **Improve accuracy** especially during temperature fluctuations

## Tips

- **Enable this feature** for the most accurate fermentation tracking
- **Works best** when the Hydrom is floating freely in the fermenter
- **No additional calibration** is needed after enabling temperature compensation
- **Check occasionally** that the Hydrom remains fully submerged as fermentation progresses

Temperature compensation takes the guesswork out of accounting for temperature variations and helps you make better decisions about your fermentation.
