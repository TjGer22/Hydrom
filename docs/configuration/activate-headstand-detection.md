# Activate Headstand Detection

## What is Headstand Detection?

During fermentation, your Hydrom needs to float normally in your fermenter. However, sometimes the Hydrom may accidentally end up upside down—for example, during cleaning, when removing it from the fermenter, or if something bumps it.

**Headstand detection** automatically recognizes when the Hydrom is inverted and stops transmitting false readings. This protects your fermentation data by preventing inaccurate measurements and saves battery power by not sending bad data.

!!! info
    Detection threshold is approximately 90 degrees. When inverted, the Hydrom will suspend measurement and transmission. It automatically resumes normal operation on the next wake cycle when placed right-side up.

## How to Enable Headstand Detection

### Step 1: Open the Menu

Open the Hydrom web interface in your browser. Look for the **menu icon** (three horizontal lines) in the top-left corner of the screen.

### Step 2: Navigate to Settings

Tap or click on **Settings** from the menu options.

### Step 3: Enable Headstand Detection

In the Settings page, locate the **Headstand Detection** toggle switch. Tap or click to enable it.

### Step 4: Save Your Settings

Click the **Save** button to apply the setting. You'll see a confirmation message.

## How It Works

When **Headstand Detection** is enabled:

- **Monitors orientation** of the Hydrom continuously
- **Detects inversion** when tilted beyond approximately 90 degrees
- **Stops transmissions** if upside down to prevent false readings
- **Prevents battery drain** from transmitting bad data
- **Auto-recovers** when the Hydrom returns to normal floating position

## Benefits

- **Protects data integrity** during handling and cleaning
- **Saves battery** by not transmitting erroneous measurements
- **Automatic recovery** — no manual intervention needed
- **Works silently** in the background

## When to Use This Feature

Enable Headstand Detection if:

- Your Hydrom occasionally gets bumped or moved around
- You clean your fermenter while the Hydrom is present
- You want the maximum battery life
- You're concerned about accidental inversions affecting your data

## Tips

- **Always leave enabled** for normal operation
- **No impact on accuracy** when the Hydrom is floating normally
- **Complements temperature compensation** for the best measurement experience
- **Check orientation occasionally** to ensure normal floating position
