# Getting Started with Hydrom

## Welcome to Hydrom

Hydrom is an intelligent, free-floating hydrometer that measures beer and wine fermentation density via Wi-Fi. Unlike traditional hydrometers, Hydrom continuously monitors gravity changes without disturbing your fermentation process, and automatically sends data to your smartphone or computer.

### What You'll Need

Before starting, gather the following:

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Hydrom device** | The smart hydrometer |
| **USB-C cable + charger** | To charge the built-in battery |
| **Smartphone or computer** | To access the web interface and monitor fermentation |
| **Wi-Fi network** | For Hydrom to connect and transmit data |
| **Wi-Fi credentials** | Your network name (SSID) and password |

## Setup Steps in Detail

### 1. Read Safety Warnings
Before handling your Hydrom, please read the [Safety Warnings](warnings-read-before-use.md) to understand important precautions, especially regarding battery safety.

**Time required:** 5 minutes

### 2. Disinfect Before Use
It's important to sterilize your Hydrom before first use. Follow the [Disinfection Guide](disinfect-before-use.md) to ensure hygienic operation.

**Time required:** 10 minutes

### 3. Charge the Battery
Your Hydrom arrives with a depleted battery. Charge it fully using the included USB-C cable before first use.

**Time required:** 2-3 hours

### 4. Connect to Wi-Fi
Configure your Hydrom to join your home or brewery Wi-Fi network. The device creates its own temporary network during setup.

**Time required:** 5 minutes

### 5. Access the Web Interface
Once connected to Wi-Fi, access the Hydrom web interface from any browser to view live measurements and configure settings.

**Time required:** 2 minutes

### 6. Calibrate Your Device
Calibration ensures accurate measurements. Choose from three methods depending on your accuracy requirements and available time.

**Time required:** 5-120 minutes (depending on method)

### 7. Configure Settings
Enable optional features like temperature compensation and headstand detection to get the most out of your Hydrom.

**Time required:** 5 minutes

### 8. Connect Services
Integrate Hydrom with popular brewing services like Brewfather, BeerSmith, or Fermentrack to track fermentation alongside other data.

**Time required:** 5-10 minutes

## Typical Fermentation Workflow

1. **Brew day:** Pitch your yeast
2. **Day 1:** Place Hydrom in fermenter and enable Wi-Fi connection
3. **Days 2-7:** Monitor gravity drop in real-time
4. **When ready:** Check final gravity to confirm fermentation is complete
5. **Packaging:** Remove Hydrom, clean, and prepare for next batch

## Tips for Best Results

- **Keep Wi-Fi stable** — Position fermenter where Wi-Fi signal is strong
- **Full submersion** — Ensure Hydrom floats freely without touching walls or lid
- **Regular monitoring** — Check the web interface daily to track progress
- **Temperature stability** — Consistent fermentation temperature improves accuracy
- **Calibration first** — Calibrate before your first use for best accuracy
