# Safety Warnings — Please Read Before Use

!!! danger "Battery Safety"
    The Hydrom contains a lithium-ion battery. Improper handling can cause fire, explosion, or chemical burns. Read and follow all warnings carefully.

## Important Safety Warnings

### 1. DO NOT Disassemble the Device

**Never** open or disassemble the Hydrom casing. The device is sealed for waterproofing and contains a lithium-ion battery that can be dangerous if exposed.

- Disassembly voids the warranty
- Opening the casing may damage the device
- Battery exposure creates fire/explosion hazards

### 2. DO NOT Damage the Battery

**Never** puncture, crush, bend, or damage the battery or casing. A damaged battery can catch fire or explode.

- Do not drop from height
- Do not apply excessive pressure
- Inspect the device regularly for dents or damage
- Do not use if the casing is cracked or leaking

### 3. DO NOT Overcharge or Use Improper Chargers

**Never** charge with a charger other than the provided USB-C cable and approved charger.

- Use only USB-C chargers rated 5V or higher
- Do not leave charging unattended for extended periods
- Disconnect when fully charged
- Do not charge in wet environments

### 4. DO NOT Expose to Extreme Temperatures

**Never** expose the device to temperatures below -10°C or above 60°C (14°F to 140°F).

- Do not leave in direct sunlight
- Do not place near heat sources
- Do not freeze the device
- Allow to acclimate to room temperature before use

### 5. DO NOT Submerge Fully Beyond Intended Depth

While Hydrom is water-resistant, it is designed for floating in fermentation vessels, not full submersion beyond normal operational depth.

- Do not push underwater or hold submerged
- Allow normal floating operation
- Do not use in high-pressure environments (deep water)
- Do not submerge the USB-C charging port

### 6. NEVER Ignore Warning Signs

**Stop using immediately if:**

- The device smells unusual or produces smoke
- The casing is hot to the touch
- The battery is swollen or deformed
- The device no longer charges properly
- There are visible cracks or leaks
- The device was dropped into water from height

If any of these occur, remove the device to a safe location away from flammable materials and contact support@hydrom.io immediately.

## Safe Usage Practices

- **Keep dry between uses** — Store in a dry location
- **Regular inspection** — Check the device weekly for damage
- **Clean appropriately** — Wipe with a damp cloth; avoid soaking before charging
- **Proper storage** — Store at room temperature in a dry place
- **Keep away from children** — Small parts and battery hazards
- **Respect the product** — Handle with care as you would electronics

## Emergency Procedures

**If battery catches fire:**
1. Do NOT attempt to extinguish with water
2. Evacuate the area immediately
3. Allow the fire to burn in open air or use a Class D fire extinguisher
4. Call emergency services (911 in US)

**If battery leaks:**
1. Do NOT touch the liquid
2. Wash affected skin with water immediately
3. Remove contaminated clothing
4. Seek medical attention if skin/eyes irritated
5. Properly dispose of the device at an e-waste recycling facility

## Warranty and Liability

Failure to follow these safety warnings:
- Voids your warranty
- May result in injury or property damage
- Releases the manufacturer from liability
- May be illegal in your jurisdiction

## Questions About Safety?

If you have questions about safe use, contact support@hydrom.io before using the device.
